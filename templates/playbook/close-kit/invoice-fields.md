# Invoice fields

> Not tax advice. Invoicing and sales-tax rules differ by country and region (for example,
> VAT/GST numbers, required wording, sequential numbering). Check with an accountant.

Every invoice should show:

| Field | Example (fictional) |
|---|---|
| The word "Invoice" | Invoice |
| Invoice number (sequential, unique) | 2026-0007 |
| Issue date | 2026-01-14 |
| Due date / payment terms | Due on receipt · or 7 days |
| Your legal name, business name, address | {Studio legal name}, {address} |
| Your tax / business registration number, if registered | {VAT/GST/tax ID} |
| Client legal name and address | {Client legal name}, {address} |
| Client tax number (B2B, where required) | {client VAT ID} |
| Description of work | "Website, Growth plan: 50% deposit to book build slot" |
| Quantity, unit price, line total | 1 × {amount} |
| Subtotal | {amount} |
| Tax rate and amount, if you charge tax | {rate}% · {amount} |
| Total due, with currency | {total} {currency} |
| Payment methods and details | Bank transfer: {account details} · Payment link: {link} |
| Reference to quote on payment | Invoice number |
| Agreement reference | "Per Website Agreement dated {date}" |
| Late payment terms (optional) | {as allowed locally} |

Issue two invoices per project: the deposit on signing, the balance on approval. Issue a
separate recurring invoice (or subscription) for the care plan.
