# Close-ready kit

Everything you need so a "yes" on a call becomes a deposit on the same call.
Prepare it once, before your first call. Replace every `{placeholder}`.

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| `agreement.md` | One-page website agreement |
| `onboarding-form.md` | Questions to send after the deposit (logo, photos, hours, logins) |
| `invoice-fields.md` | What every invoice should show |
| `deposit-instructions.md` | The message you send with payment details |

**Not legal or tax advice.** These are plain-language starting points. Contract, consumer,
tax and invoicing rules differ by country and region. Have a local professional review
your versions before you use them, especially the agreement and invoice.
