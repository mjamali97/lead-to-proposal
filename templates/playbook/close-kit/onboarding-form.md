# Onboarding form

Send right after the deposit (as a Google Form, Typeform, or a plain email). Short beats
complete: ask for what you need to start, then fill gaps on a 15-minute call.

## About the business
1. Business name exactly as it should appear
2. Main phone number (the one the "call" button should ring)
3. Email for enquiries / form submissions
4. Address, or service area if you go to customers
5. Opening hours (and holiday hours)
6. Your main services, most important first (up to 6)
7. Who are your best customers? What do they usually ask before they book?
8. What makes you different from the next company? (in your words)
9. Licences, certifications, guarantees, years in business (only what's true and current)

## Brand and content
10. Logo file (the highest quality you have: SVG, PDF, or large PNG)
11. Brand colours, if you have them (otherwise we use the colours from your logo and site)
12. 5–15 photos of your work, team, premises or products (originals from your phone are fine)
13. Reviews or testimonials you'd like featured (we can also use your public Google reviews)
14. Anything that must NOT appear on the site

## Access
15. Domain: where is it registered? (We'll guide you to add us or update settings; never
    send passwords by email: use the registrar's "invite" feature or a password manager share)
16. Current website login, if it has content you want to keep
17. Google Business Profile: add {studio email} as a manager (if part of your plan)
18. Social media links to show on the site

## Practical
19. Best time for the review call
20. Who approves the final site?
21. Launch date wishes or deadlines (season, event, opening)

Thank you. We start building as soon as we have items 1–6, 10 and 12.
