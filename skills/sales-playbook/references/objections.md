# Objections

Each answer acknowledges, adds one true fact, and goes back to a booked time. Don't argue.
Record the objection in the CRM; recurring ones become disqualify rules or offer changes.

### "Just send me the proposal / sample."
> "I've sent you the picture of the page. A PDF can't tell me what you'd change, though.
> Let's look at it together for 10 minutes. Tomorrow at 8 or at 7 in the evening?"

If they insist, send the proposal PDF **and** book a time to "go through it together"
before you hang up.

### "Call me in the evening / later."
> "Sure. 7:15 or 8:00?"

Never accept "evening" without a time. Confirm by message right away: "Talk at 7:15, {You}."

### "I already have a website."
> "I looked at it before I called. {One specific finding, e.g. the phone number can't be
> tapped on a phone.} That's exactly what the new page fixes. Can I show you side by side?"

### "I'm building it myself."
> "Good. Use this page as your blueprint. If it's not live in three weeks, call me and
> we'll finish it in {delivery_days} days."

Put a check-in date three weeks out. Many DIY sites never launch. One check-in, then move on.

### "We already have someone / a team."
> "Great, then you're covered. Do you know another business owner who doesn't have anyone?
> I give {referral reward} for every one who signs."

Then move on.

### "It's too expensive."
> "What's one job worth to you? … So it pays for itself with {n} jobs. If the budget is
> tight, start with {entry plan} now and add the rest later."

**Lower the scope, never the price.** Offer a split payment (deposit now, balance at launch).

### "Business is slow / paused right now."
> "When do you expect it to pick up? Let's have the page ready two weeks before that."

Put that date in the CRM. One useful message a month until then.

### "I work for someone else."
> "Who should I speak with, the owner? Could you introduce me, or may I mention your name?"

### "I need to think about it / talk to my partner."
> "Of course. What's the one thing you'd want to be sure about? … Let's put 10 minutes in
> with {partner} too. Thursday at 6 or Friday at 8?"

### "How did you get my number?"
> "It's the number on your Google listing / website. I'm a local web designer and I built a
> page for you before calling. If you'd rather I didn't call again, I'll note that now."

If they say stop: `l2p queue --stop <phone>` and apologise once.

### "Is this a scam? / Who are you?"
> "Fair question. I'm {You}, {Studio}, {city}. You can see my work at {studio site}. You
> don't pay anything until you've seen the page and agreed. Even then it's a deposit, and
> you get it back if you don't like the finished page."

This is why the studio's own site must show examples and prices (see `everything-else.md`).

### "Can you just give me the link / files?"
> "The page goes live on your domain once we start. Until then I share my screen so you
> can see it working."
